# ai-assisted

Everything in this repository that an AI made, kept in one place, so anyone can see what was AI work and decide for themselves what they think of it.

- **One folder per AI tool.** So far only `claude/` (Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic).
- **Not part of the game.** Nothing here is the game or its learning content; that is made by people (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **Decisions are human.** The AI researched, drafted and built; the choices were made by annaothercrow, the maintainer. Each folder says who did what.
- **Left out of exports.** Every export preset leaves out `ai-assisted/*`.

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| [claude/learning/](claude/learning/README.md) | Notes and code examples for learning Godot and programming, including the first graybox. |
| [claude/research/](claude/research/README.md) | The research behind finished board cards, with a decision note for each, and the learner profile the research was written for. |

Until 14.11.2026 the learning folder was called `ai-learning/` at the top of the repository.
