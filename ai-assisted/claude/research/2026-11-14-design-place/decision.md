# Design place: decision

Card: **Design place: Excalidraw boards in design/** on the [ACE Workshop board](https://github.com/orgs/An-na-other-Context/projects/1) (Done, 14.11.2026).
Research: [research.html](research.html) (round 2, the latest version; download it and open it in a browser). Written for the learner profile [annaothercrow](../learner-profiles/annaothercrow.md).

## Decision (annaothercrow, 13.11.2026)

Excalidraw, with the boards in [ace-workshop/design/](../../../../design/README.md).

## Why (annaothercrow's reasons)

- a visual pinboard: more visual, less text, short notes in different media, all at a glance
- a mind-map / brainstorm document, structured by connecting information
- a visual editor, in the browser and in VS Code

## What the research showed (AI, [research.html](research.html))

- No tool surely met both must-haves (works offline; voice or video notes on the board).
- Excalidraw: works offline, open source (MIT), arrows that attach to shapes, open JSON files; no audio or video on the board.

## Trade-off accepted

Voice and video notes live in [design/media/](../../../../design/media/README.md), named like a card on the board.

## Turned down

GitHub wiki (not flexible enough), GitHub Pages (effort; kept for hosting the product later), Obsidian (the round-1 recommendation), draw.io (too diagram-like).
