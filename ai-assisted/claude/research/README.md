# research

The research behind finished cards on the [ACE Workshop board](https://github.com/orgs/An-na-other-Context/projects/1): for each card, the latest research page and a note on what was decided and why. Part of [ai-assisted/](../../README.md).

> **Made by AI.** The research pages and notes here were written by Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, with research helpers (other Claude instances) looking up sources online. Every research page lists each source with what was actually read, seen only as a summary, or blocked. The decisions are annaothercrow's.

## Why it's here

So the reasoning behind each choice can be traced, and it's clear what was AI work. Anyone can look at the research, see how AI was used, and decide for themselves where they agree or would choose differently, even if it's only about tooling.

## What's in each folder

One folder per finished card, named `YYYY-MM-DD-<card>/`:

- `research.html`: the latest version of the research page. In this repo copy, wording that said "you" points to the learner profile instead; findings, sources and numbers are unchanged.
- `decision.md`: what was decided, by whom, why, and what was turned down.

## How to read the pages

GitHub shows `.html` files as code. To read a page: download it and open it in a browser. Viewing them online (GitHub Pages) comes later; it's a card on the board.

Godot skips this folder (the empty file `.gdignore`).
