## What

Wants to understand how computers and operating systems work, from the hardware up. Background in game design; knows Unity, now learning Godot.

## When

Learning alone, outside school or a course, in sessions of varying energy; some days there is little.

## Where

At a PC. A real workshop is out of reach for now (access and cost), so it is emulated on screen.

## Why

Top-down learning doesn't work: without the lowest layer, the learner gets stuck in abstraction.

## How

Bottom up, "dive and climb": down layer by layer, asking why, when, where, who, what and how; checking "still on track?"; back up, applying each layer. Pulled towards side tracks; thinks visually, in short connected notes.

## Fit with ACE Workshop

The design line isn't settled yet. The idea so far: the workshop starts at the bottom layer and builds upward, because top-down learning doesn't work for this learner.
